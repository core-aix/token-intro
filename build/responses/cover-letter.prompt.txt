About the role

You will work closely with branch managers, volunteers, local schools, and voluntary sector partners, and you will play a central role in helping us reach people who do not currently use our services.

- Collect and analyse participation data, and produce quarterly reports for our board
- Support funding applications by providing evidence of impact and community need
- Represent the library service at local partnership meetings and public events

- A genuine commitment to widening access and reducing barriers to participation

Desirable criteria
