    About these numbers
    • Every question here was really sent to Claude Haiku 4.5. The answers are its genuine, unedited replies.
    • Costs use that model's published rates — $1 per million tokens read, $5 per million written — converted at roughly £1 = $1.27.
    • Tokens are counted with a widely used open tokeniser. Every model chops text up a little differently; here it came within about 3% of the model's own count.
    • Models also produce hidden thinking tokens, which are charged too. These figures count only the words you can see, so a real bill would be higher.
    • Prepared 2026. Prices change quickly.
